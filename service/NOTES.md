# service/: « Édition »

**Direction.** The studio as a small publisher's catalogue: Swiss-editorial grid, with a literary serif voice.

**Fonts.**
- Newsreader, on the opsz axis: 72 for display, italics for emphasis, oldstyle figures in body text.
- Schibsted Grotesk for text and UI.

**Palette.**

| Colour | Hex | Contrast on paper |
|---|---|---|
| Paper | `#F3F0E8` | — |
| Ink | `#141414` | 16.2:1 |
| Graphite | `#5A564E` | 6.4:1 |
| Vermilion | `#D23A1C` | 4.2:1, large text and marks only |
| Vermilion (small text) | `#B5301A` | 5.4:1 |

**Signature elements.**
- A running head that shows the current section.
- Sticky `§` folios in the margin.
- Footnotes for each section, with back links and a highlight on the note you jump to.
- A giant « 290 » that wipes in.
- A natural-language letter form.
- A specimen card that follows the pointer over the catalogue index.
- A print stylesheet.

**Facts.** Only those in `_brief/service-facts.md`.

**Form.** The form is not wired. On submit it shows an honest notice and a prefilled mailto link.

**Mentions légales.** All legal content is kept. Two sentences that had become inaccurate were corrected: the one about Google Maps embeds, and one that did not mention the aperçus.
